<objective>
Replace the proprietary Vapi dependency with a native Twilio Voice + Groq LLM interactive speech qualification agent.

Purpose: Eliminates the Vapi paywall and credit-card gatekeeping by using standard Twilio telephony (trial-credit friendly) paired with free Groq LLM conversational intelligence. Outbound calls dial real phone numbers, converse naturally via TwiML speech recognition and neural TTS, and forward the complete transcript into the deterministic qualification state machine.
Output: Production-ready outbound voice agent endpoints and provider methods in `backend/providers.py` and `backend/server.py`.
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Implement /api/voice/twiml and /api/voice/gather Interactive Speech Endpoints</name>
  <files>backend/server.py</files>
  <read_first>backend/server.py, backend/providers.py</read_first>
  <action>
    Implement the TwiML speech handling webhook endpoints in `backend/server.py`:
    1. `POST /api/voice/twiml`:
       - Accepts `lead_id` as query parameter or form data.
       - Retrieves lead details (`name`) from `db.leads`.
       - Returns a TwiML XML response with Amazon Polly voice greeting (e.g. `Polly.Joanna`):
         `"Hi {name}, this is Sarah from EstateX Realty. Thanks for your inquiry! Do you have a quick moment to discuss what you're looking for in a home?"`
       - Includes `<Gather input="speech" action="/api/voice/gather?lead_id={lead_id}&step=1" method="POST" speechTimeout="auto" timeout="6">`.
    2. `POST /api/voice/gather`:
       - Extracts `SpeechResult` from the Twilio webhook form payload.
       - Appends speech turn `{"role": "lead", "text": speech_result}` to lead's conversation history in `db.leads`.
       - Calls Groq LLM (using `providers.llm_json` or active Groq model) to generate the next conversational real-estate qualification reply. The prompt directs the agent to qualify: timeline, budget range, mortgage pre-approval status, and preferred neighbourhoods.
       - If further qualification is needed (under 4 turns), returns `<Response><Say voice="Polly.Joanna">{agent_reply}</Say><Gather input="speech" action="/api/voice/gather?lead_id={lead_id}&step={step+1}" method="POST" speechTimeout="auto" timeout="6"/></Response>`.
       - If qualification is complete or caller says goodbye, returns `<Response><Say voice="Polly.Joanna">Thank you! I have recorded your preferences and our senior broker will follow up shortly. Have a wonderful day!</Say><Hangup/></Response>`, stores the full transcript, and schedules/dispatches `qualify_and_route(lead_id)`.
  </action>
  <verify>backend/.venv/bin/pytest backend/tests/</verify>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - `POST /api/voice/twiml` returns valid `application/xml` containing `<Gather input="speech">`.
    - `POST /api/voice/gather` accumulates transcript turns and invokes `qualify_and_route(lead_id)` at call completion.
  </acceptance_criteria>
