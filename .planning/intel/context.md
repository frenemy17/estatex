# Extracted Supporting Context

## Real-Estate Industry Problem & Speed-to-Lead
- source: docs/PRD.md
- context: Real-estate leads go cold in minutes; the first responder wins. Humans cannot call every lead instantly 24/7 or qualify consistently. The platform automates instant outbound qualification and calendar booking while remaining honest and inspectable when integrations fail.

## Target Personas
- source: docs/PRD.md
- context:
  - Real-estate broker/team lead: Monitors the pipeline, approves escalations, reviews AI qualifications, inspects live vs mock integration health.
  - End buyer/renter: Fills out capture forms or submits Google Ads Lead Forms expecting prompt follow-up.

## Evaluation & Simulation Harness
- source: docs/PRD.md
- context: Scripted test harness with 15 simulated leads and `/api/eval` measuring rubric agreement, booking rate, hallucination rate, and baseline keyword performance.
